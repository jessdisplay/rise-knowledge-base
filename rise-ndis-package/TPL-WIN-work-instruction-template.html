[Work Instruction Title]

Document type: Work Instruction. One task, one page, written for the person doing it right now. Worked example: WIN-INC-01 Responding to an Incident (frontline).

How to use this template. A work instruction is the pocket version of one part of a procedure. Simplest language in the suite — short sentences, "you" voice, no citations in the body. If it will not fit on one page, it belongs in the procedure.

3. When to use this

[One sentence.] Example: Something has happened, or nearly happened, that harmed or could have harmed a participant.

4. What you do

[Numbered actions, imperative voice, one action per line. Aim for 5–9 steps.]

Example:

  1. Keep the participant safe. Move away from danger. Call 000 if anyone needs urgent help.
  2. Stay calm and stay with the participant if you can.
  3. Tell your Service Manager before your shift ends — phone, do not wait for email.
  4. Write down what you saw while it is fresh: what happened, when, who was there.
  5. Start the Incident Report Form (FRM-INC-01) with your manager.
  6. Do not discuss the incident with anyone outside the team.
  7. Ask for support if you are affected — that is what the debrief is for.

5. Stop and escalate if…

[The red-flag box. 2–4 bullets: conditions that mean stop following this sheet and call the named role immediately.]

Example: the participant needs medical attention · the incident may involve abuse, neglect or a restrictive practice · you are unsure whether it counts — report anyway.
