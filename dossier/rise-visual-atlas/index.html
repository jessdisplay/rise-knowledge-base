Key — how to read this atlas

Colour language (consistent across all twelve figures)

Purple — the law: Acts and legislative rules
Teal — framework content: standards and their quality indicators
Coral — provider material: policies, procedures, records, evidence
Pink — the external check: auditors and the Commission
Grey — neutral or structural: Parliament; not-applicable outcomes
White — questions and plain items within a flow
Green — pass: conformity, covered
Amber — warning: proof gap (paper without evidence)
Red — gap: documentation gap, non-conformity

Evidence-status labels (bolded in every caption)

Verified — checked directly against the instruments or primary sources on the dates in the session log (rise-dossier-index.md).

Teaching model — a faithful simplification built to explain; not a structure the law itself declares.

Analysis — a synthesis of verified material; sound but interpretive.

Example — invented illustrative content, always labelled inside the figure.

Mixed — the figure combines the above; its caption or legend states which parts are which.
