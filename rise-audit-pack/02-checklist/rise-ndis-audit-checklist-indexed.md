## 10. Cross-cutting evidence-hygiene layer (applies to every standard)

**The "seven recurring demands"** your prior read of all 66 standards surfaced — the indicators keep asking for the same seven things `[V-prior]`:
1. a policy or documented process;
2. communication in language the person understands;
3. consent;
4. trained workers **with records**;
5. documented plans that get **reviewed**;
6. **records/registers** of what happened;
7. compliance with a **named companion rulebook** (Incident, Complaints, Restrictive Practices, or SDA Conditions rules).

**Cross-cutting auditor behaviours to design one-click graph queries for** `[V-now / C]`:
- registers before prose; **dates compared** (screening vs start date; incident vs entry; notification vs deadline);
- **version footers** checked against the master register;
- workers asked to **describe** a procedure, answer compared to the document;
- participants interviewed with **opt-out sampling** (Commission-verified);
- the evidence trail **walked in both directions** (policy→evidence and evidence→policy).

**Evidence-hygiene checklist (every document):** version-numbered + dated · policy has matching procedure + form/register · forms have completed instances on file · registers are live · legislative references current · staff can articulate it · participants confirm it · a policy-to-standard map exists and is current · self-assessment done against the **current** standards · everything scaled to **size, scale, scope and complexity**. `[D, distilled from the real report]`
