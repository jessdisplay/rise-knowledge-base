Purpose

This policy states [Provider Name]'s commitments for access to supports, meeting CORE-3.1 Access to supports.

Scope

Applies to all workers, key personnel, volunteers and contractors of [Provider Name], across the services named in our registration. [Adjust if narrower.]

Policy statements

  1. Who we can support, and any entry criteria and costs, are clear and communicated accessibly.
  2. Access decisions are non-discriminatory and recorded.
  3. Waiting arrangements are transparent and reviewed.
  4. We do not refuse or withdraw supports just because a participant makes an informed dignity-of-risk choice.
