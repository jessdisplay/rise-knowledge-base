How to use this map

  1. Every box is one thing. A law. A rule. A document.
  2. Lines join things that belong together.
  3. Up means "why". The law is at the top.
  4. Down means "how and proof". Documents are at the bottom.
  5. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Drag to move.
  6. Hold or hover on a box to read what it is.
  7. Tap a box. Its connections light up. Everything else fades.
  8. The words on the lit lines say why things connect.
  9. Tap empty space to see everything again.
  10. The Path at the top shows where you have been.
  11. Nothing is locked. You cannot break anything.

These words are drafts. Real wording gets tested with real users - that is a build rule.

Key

Purple - the law: the Act, the Rules, the rulebooks, and adjacent Acts.

Teal - the framework: modules and all 70 standards, SIL included with register-verified titles.

Coral - the provider layer: all 123 documents from the register.

Pink - the check: audits, findings, improvement (a design layer - the four pink nodes and their five edges are not part of the register's counts).

Solid border - register verification V, or verified this session. Dashed border - register verification C, or an instrument sighted by ID but not fetched: re-verify before load-bearing use.

Solid line - a recorded edge from the seed graph. Dashed line - the design-layer audit edges only. Every provider edge on this map is real.

Line words (shown when a box is tapped): IMPLEMENTS exists to satisfy that rule - OPERATIONALISES how we actually do it - USES the form it fills in - RECORDS_TO where records land - RELATES_TO other law it must follow - PART_OF belongs inside - MADE_UNDER gets its power from - ASSESSES what the audit checks - ADDRESSES the fix for a finding.

Rendered by rise_map_build.py from rise-nodes-and-edges.json (224 nodes / 528 edges) - the map is an output, never hand-edited; design law 1 honoured. SIL standards carry register-verified titles; their Rules clauses are TBC in the register itself. Indicator items arrive with the Milestone 2 parser. The numbered background bands match the annotated meta-model (v0.2): 1 legislation · 2 practice standards · 3 provider documents · 4 proof · 5 the check. When a box is tapped, bright means its direct connections (with their words); soft glow means the full thread — up through module, framework and Rules to the Act, down its document chain, and across to the audit loop.
