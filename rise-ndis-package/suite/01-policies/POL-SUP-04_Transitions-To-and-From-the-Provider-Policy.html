Purpose

This policy states [Provider Name]'s commitments for transitions to and from the provider, meeting CORE-3.5 Transitions to and from a provider.

Scope

Applies to all workers, key personnel, volunteers and contractors of [Provider Name], across the services named in our registration. [Adjust if narrower.]

Policy statements

  1. Entries, exits and transitions are planned with the participant and the people they choose.
  2. Information transfers to new providers only with consent, using FRM-SUP-04.
  3. Supports are never withdrawn abruptly or punitively.
  4. Transition risks are assessed and managed.
