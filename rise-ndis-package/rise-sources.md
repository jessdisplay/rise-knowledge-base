# Rise — Source Register

**v0.1 — compiled 5 July 2026.** Every external claim in the Rise NDIS package traced to
its source. Companion to `rise-methodology.md` (which explains *how* sources are used)
and the Sources section of `rise-dossier.html`. All URLs accessed 5 July 2026 unless
noted. Legislation cited in the pattern of the *Australian Guide to Legal Citation*
(4th ed, 2018 — edition currency is training knowledge, moderate-high confidence;
verify before formal legal use).

**The rule these sources serve:** a fact only carries the "Verified" stamp if it rests
on a Tier 1 source. Lower tiers can corroborate, alert, and point — never verify alone.

## How to read a claim in this package

Find the claim's stamp (V/C/T/D) in the register or dossier → this document tells you
which tier stands behind that stamp → the methodology document tells you what evidence
would move it up a rung. If a claim here ever conflicts with the instrument on the
Federal Register, the instrument wins.
