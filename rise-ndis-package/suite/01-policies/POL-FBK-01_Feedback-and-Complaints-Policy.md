## Purpose

This policy states [Provider Name]'s commitments for feedback and complaints, meeting CORE-2.5 Complaints management and resolution; VER-3 Complaints management and resolution.

## Scope

Applies to all workers, key personnel, volunteers and contractors of [Provider Name], across the services named in our registration. [Adjust if narrower.]

## Policy statements

1. Feedback and complaints are welcome, easy to give, and can be made with support or anonymously.
2. No one is treated worse for complaining.
3. Complaints are acknowledged quickly and resolved fairly, with procedural fairness for everyone involved.
4. We always tell people about external options, including the NDIS Commission.
5. Every complaint is recorded in REG-FBK-01 and feeds our improvement register.
