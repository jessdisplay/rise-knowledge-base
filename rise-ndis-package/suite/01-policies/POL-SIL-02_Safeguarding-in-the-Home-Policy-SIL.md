## Policy statements

1. A SIL house is the participant's home first; safeguards protect without controlling.
2. Home risks are assessed with participants, respecting dignity of risk (PRO-SIL-02).
3. Concerns about safety, abuse or neglect escalate immediately under POL-SGD-01 and the incident system.
4. Participants shape house rules and routines.
