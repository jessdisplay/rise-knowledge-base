## What you do

1. Keep the participant safe. Move away from danger. Call 000 if anyone needs urgent help.
2. Stay calm and stay with the participant if you can.
3. Tell your Service Manager before your shift ends - phone, do not wait for email.
4. Write down what you saw while it is fresh: what happened, when, who was there.
5. Start the Incident Report Form (FRM-INC-01) with your manager.
6. Do not discuss the incident outside the team.
7. Ask for support if you are affected - that is what the debrief is for.
