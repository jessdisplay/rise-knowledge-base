Every rule has an address

Sch 7 · cl 4s 69

The purple half says where the rule lives: Schedule 7 (attachment number 7 at the back of the Rules 2018), clause 4 (numbered paragraph 4). The teal half says where its checklist lives: section 69 of the Quality Indicators Guidelines — the things an auditor looks for. The pairing isn't a guess: the law itself contains a table matching every clause to its section.

Why some rules have two names

The two documents were written by different hands. Eight rules carry a slightly different title in each — one says "dwellings" where the other says "Properties". Both are official. Rise keeps both and tells you which document each name comes from.

Why the version date matters

Laws get amended. A "compilation" is the stitched-together current version. The Rules themselves say the checklist "as existing from time to time" applies — which means the current version always wins. Anything you read carries the date it was checked, and this framework changed twice in 2026 alone.

How an auditor reads it

They read the clause, open the matching section, and turn each checklist line into a question: show me the document, let me talk to your people, let me sample your records. Five steps, drawn in the atlas.
