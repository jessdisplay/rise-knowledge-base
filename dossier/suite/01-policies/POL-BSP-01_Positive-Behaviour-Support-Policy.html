Purpose

This policy states [Provider Name]'s commitments for positive behaviour support, meeting M2A-1 Behaviour support in the NDIS; M2-1 Behaviour support in the NDIS.

Scope

Applies to all workers, key personnel, volunteers and contractors of [Provider Name], across the services named in our registration. [Adjust if narrower.]

Policy statements

  1. We practise positive behaviour support and work to reduce and eliminate restrictive practices.
  2. A regulated restrictive practice is used only when authorised, in a current behaviour support plan, as a last resort, least restrictive, for the shortest time.
  3. Every use is recorded (FRM-BSP-01) and reported to the Commission as required, including monthly reporting.
  4. Behaviour support plans are implemented as written, by trained workers, and reviewed.
  5. Unauthorised use of a restrictive practice is a reportable incident.

Roles and responsibilities

Owner: Quality Manager. All managers apply this policy in their services; all workers follow it and report concerns. [Add roles specific to your structure.]
