## Scope

## Policy statements

1. Each participant directs their own supports; we fit around their choices, not the reverse.
2. Culture, identity, values and beliefs are respected in how supports are delivered.
3. We communicate in each participant's preferred language, mode and pace.
4. We actively support access to advocates, interpreters and communication aids.
