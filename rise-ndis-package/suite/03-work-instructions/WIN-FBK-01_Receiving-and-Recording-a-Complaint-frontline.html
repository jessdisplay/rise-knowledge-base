When to use this

What you do

  1. Thank the person - feedback is welcome here.
  2. Listen. Do not defend or explain yet.
  3. Write down their words as close as you can.
  4. Ask how they would like it fixed, and if they want an advocate.
  5. Give it to the Quality Manager today using FRM-FBK-01.
  6. Never treat anyone differently for complaining.
