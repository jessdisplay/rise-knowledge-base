## Policy statements

1. Health and safety duties are owned from the top and resourced properly.
2. Workers are consulted on things that affect their health and safety.
3. Hazards, injuries and near misses are reported and acted on - psychosocial hazards included.
4. WHS obligations are managed alongside, never instead of, participant safety.
