Reconciliation: Commission Booklet (Nov 2021, v4) × Rise Indexed Checklist

Version 0.1 — 6 July 2026 · Supersedes the gap tables in rise-ndis-audit-checklist-indexed.md §§3–5 and coverage map v1.

1. Source appraisal — what was uploaded

Verified [V-now]: NDIS Practice Standards and Quality Indicators, NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission, November 2021, Version 4. Contains every outcome and its quality indicators for the Core module, six supplementary modules, and the Verification module, plus definitions.

What it is not: - Not the legislative instrument. The booklet itself states the outcomes sit in the PRPS Rules 2018 and the indicators in the Quality Indicators Guidelines 2018. It reproduces content in guidance form and carries no Schedule/clause or section numbers — so it cannot verify statutory addresses. - Pre-SIL. Nov 2021 predates the 2026 SIL module entirely; the skills-descriptor definition is pinned "as existing on 1 October 2021". A post-SIL edition of this booklet may exist — not checked this session.

2. Method

  1. Name cross-validation [V-now]: every standard name in the checklist compared against the booklet's headings.
  2. Ordered-correspondence inference [I]: where a module's booklet order + count exactly matches a known QI section span, positions are paired to sections. Basis per module: exact count match (7→ss 38–44; 8→ss 47–54; 7→ss 56–62) plus anchors — M2 positions 1–3 match known s 38/39/40 names; M2A position 5 ("Monitoring and Reporting the Use of Regulated Restrictive Practices", containing the monthly-online-reporting indicator) lands on s 51, exactly where the prior session's auditor focus map placed that indicator; M3 positions 6–7 match verified s 61/s 62 names.
  3. What would falsify the inference: the Guidelines' internal order differing from the booklet's presentation order, or a non-contiguous base section within a module span. Resolved definitively by the register's Standards sheet or an FRL fetch of the Guidelines' mapping tables. Rules-side clause numbers were NOT inferred — Schedule 1 demonstrably skips clause numbers (8, 17, 23 absent), so counting clauses is unsafe.

7. Discrepancy flagged — check against the instrument

The booklet's Module 2A preamble opens: "These NDIS Practice Standards apply to NDIS providers who are registered to provide specialist behaviour support…" before extending to "providers using restrictive practices in the delivery of any NDIS supports and services." The first sentence appears to duplicate the Module 2 preamble, and it sits in tension with the prior-session finding that the Guidelines explicitly scope 2A to providers who implement plans rather than the specialists who write them. Likely a booklet editing artefact; resolve against the Guidelines text when fetched. [Unresolved — flagged, not judged.]
