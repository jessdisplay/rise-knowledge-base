Purpose

This procedure sets out how, who and when for supported decision-making.

When this procedure starts

[The trigger, in one sentence a frontline worker recognises.]

Steps

# Who What they do Record / deadline
1 House Supervisor Learn how each participant prefers to make decisions, and who they want involved FRM-SIL-01 started
2 Worker Offer real choices in daily life; give information the participant's way; allow time Daily practice
3 House Supervisor Record significant decisions and the support given - not to control, to evidence support FRM-SIL-01
4 Service Manager Review decision-support records for patterns of substitute decision-making Review note
5 House Supervisor Escalate where a participant's decision is being overridden without lawful basis POL-SGD-01 pathway

Escalation

If a step cannot be completed, or severity increases: escalate to the owner, then the CEO. Never leave a statutory deadline unowned.
