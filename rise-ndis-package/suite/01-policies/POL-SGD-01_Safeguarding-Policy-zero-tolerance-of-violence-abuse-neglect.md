## Purpose

This policy states [Provider Name]'s commitments for safeguarding (zero tolerance of violence, abuse, neglect, exploitation and discrimination), meeting CORE-1.5 Freedom from violence, abuse, neglect, exploitation or discrimination; SIL-2 Safeguarding.

## Scope

Applies to all workers, key personnel, volunteers and contractors of [Provider Name], across the services named in our registration. [Adjust if narrower.]

## Policy statements

1. We have zero tolerance for violence, abuse, neglect, exploitation and discrimination.
2. Every worker can recognise the signs and knows how to respond and report, including reportable incidents.
3. We recruit safely, supervise actively, and act on concerns early.
4. Participants are supported to speak up safely, with advocates where wanted.
5. Concerns are never minimised because of a person's disability or communication style.
