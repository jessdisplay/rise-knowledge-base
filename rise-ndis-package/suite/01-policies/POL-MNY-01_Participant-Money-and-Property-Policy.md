## Scope

## Policy statements

1. We handle participant money or property only with documented consent and a clear purpose.
2. Every transaction is recorded on FRM-MNY-01 with receipts, and logged in REG-MNY-01.
3. Verification controls apply to every transaction - no exceptions for small amounts.
4. Workers never borrow from, lend to, or benefit financially from participants.
5. The register is reconciled and reviewed on a set cycle.
