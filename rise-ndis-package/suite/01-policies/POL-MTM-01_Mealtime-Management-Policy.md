## Purpose

This policy states [Provider Name]'s commitments for mealtime management, meeting CORE-4.4 Mealtime management (added 2021).

## Scope

Applies to all workers, key personnel, volunteers and contractors of [Provider Name], across the services named in our registration. [Adjust if narrower.]

## Policy statements

1. Mealtime needs are assessed and planned for each participant who needs support to eat or drink safely (FRM-MTM-01).
2. Texture modifications and positioning are followed exactly as planned.
3. Concerns about swallowing escalate promptly, including to PRO-HID-03 where dysphagia is assessed.
4. Workers who support mealtimes are trained for it.
