## Purpose

This policy states [Provider Name]'s commitments for medication management, meeting CORE-4.3 Management of medication.

## Scope

Applies to all workers, key personnel, volunteers and contractors of [Provider Name], across the services named in our registration. [Adjust if narrower.]

## Policy statements

1. Only workers trained and assessed as competent administer or assist with medication.
2. Medication charts (FRM-MED-01) are current, complete and followed exactly.
3. PRN medication is given only per WIN-MED-01 and the participant's plan.
4. Every medication error or near miss is an incident: reported, recorded and learned from.
5. Medication is stored and disposed of safely.
