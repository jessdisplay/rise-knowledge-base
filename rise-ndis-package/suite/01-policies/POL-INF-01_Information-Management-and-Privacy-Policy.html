Purpose

This policy states [Provider Name]'s commitments for information management and privacy, meeting CORE-2.4 Information management; CORE-1.3 Privacy and dignity.

Scope

Applies to all workers, key personnel, volunteers and contractors of [Provider Name], across the services named in our registration. [Adjust if narrower.]

Policy statements

  1. We collect only the information we need, with informed consent recorded on FRM-INF-01.
  2. Information is stored securely and seen only by people who need it for their role.
  3. Participants can see and correct information about them.
  4. Suspected privacy breaches are handled immediately under PRO-INF-02, including our data-breach notification duties.
  5. Records are kept, and destroyed, according to the retention rules that apply to them.

Roles and responsibilities

Owner: Quality Manager. All managers apply this policy in their services; all workers follow it and report concerns. [Add roles specific to your structure.]
