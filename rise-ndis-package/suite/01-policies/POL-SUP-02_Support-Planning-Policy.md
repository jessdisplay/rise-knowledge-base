## Scope

Applies to all workers, key personnel, volunteers and contractors of [Provider Name], across the services named in our registration. [Adjust if narrower.]

## Policy statements

1. Every participant has a current support plan they led the development of.
2. Plans reflect preferences, goals, communication needs and assessed risks.
3. Plans are reviewed on schedule and whenever circumstances change.
4. Workers deliver to the plan, and say so when the plan needs to change.
