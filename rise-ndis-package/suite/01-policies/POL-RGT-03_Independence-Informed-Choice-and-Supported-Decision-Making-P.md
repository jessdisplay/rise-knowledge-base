## Purpose

This policy states [Provider Name]'s commitments for independence, informed choice and supported decision-making, meeting CORE-1.4 Independence and informed choice; SIL-1 Supported decision-making.

## Scope

Applies to all workers, key personnel, volunteers and contractors of [Provider Name], across the services named in our registration. [Adjust if narrower.]

## Policy statements

1. We presume every participant can make decisions, and we support decision-making rather than substitute for it.
2. Decisions are made with the participant, in their preferred way, with the people they choose.
3. Dignity of risk is respected: informed choices are supported and documented, not overridden.
4. Substitute decision-making is used only where the law provides, and recorded.
5. In SIL settings this policy operates through PRO-SIL-01 and FRM-SIL-01.
