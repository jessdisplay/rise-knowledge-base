[Form Title]

Document type: Form. What this form captures and what happens to it once completed. Worked example: FRM-INC-01 Incident Report Form.

How to use this template. A form is a capture instrument: every field must earn its place by feeding the register or a statutory notification. If no one downstream uses a field, delete it. A completed form becomes evidence — handle it per the privacy notice and the retention rule in §6.

3. Privacy notice (printed on the form)

[2–3 sentences, plain English: why we collect this, who sees it, the participant's right to access it. Reference the Information Management and Privacy Policy by ID only.]

Example: We collect this information to keep people safe and to meet our legal duties. It is only seen by the people who manage the incident, and by the NDIS Commission if the law requires us to notify them. You can ask to see information about you at any time.

4. Form fields

[Group fields into sections. For each: label · type (text / date / choice / signature) · required? A "choice" field lists its options so data stays consistent with the register columns.]

Example sections for FRM-INC-01:

Section Fields
A. The incident Date and time (date, req) · Location (text, req) · What happened (text, req) · Near miss? (Y/N, req)
B. People Participant(s) involved · Workers present · Witnesses
C. Immediate response Actions taken (text, req) · Medical help needed? (Y/N) · Participant informed and supported? (Y/N, req)
D. Assessment (manager) Severity (choice: minor / moderate / major) · Reportable incident? (choice: yes / no / unsure — if yes or unsure, go to PRO-INC-02 now)
E. Sign-off Worker signature · Manager signature · Date entered in REG-INC-01
