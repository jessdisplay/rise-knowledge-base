1. Check the chart: is this PRN authorised, and do the conditions apply right now?
2. Check right person, medication, dose, route, time.
3. Give the medication and watch for the effect the chart says to expect.
4. Record it on FRM-MED-01 immediately, with the reason.
5. If in doubt at any point - do not give it. Call your Service Manager.
6. Anything unexpected: treat as an incident and report this shift.
