Verified provisions worth knowing (paraphrased)

Section 20(3) — the registration table: 36 classes of supports, each mapped to its applicable schedules and audit type. This is the legislated "does it apply to me?" gate.

Section 24 — the quality indicators "as existing from time to time" must be taken into account in assessments: the current Guidelines always apply.

Section 13B — the mid-term audit within 18 months, always covering governance plus anything previously subject to corrective action.
