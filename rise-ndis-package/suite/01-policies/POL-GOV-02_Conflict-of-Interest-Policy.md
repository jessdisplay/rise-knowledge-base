## Policy statements

1. Everyone declares actual, potential and perceived conflicts of interest using FRM-GOV-01.
2. Declared conflicts are recorded in REG-GOV-02 and managed or removed before decisions are made.
3. Support coordination and SDA conflicts are handled under the specific module requirements.
4. No worker or key person may gain personally from a relationship with a participant.
5. The conflict register is reviewed by leadership on a set cycle.
