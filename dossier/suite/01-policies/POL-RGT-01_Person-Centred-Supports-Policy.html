Purpose

This policy states [Provider Name]'s commitments for person-centred supports, meeting CORE-1.1 Person-centred supports; CORE-1.2 Individual values and beliefs.

Scope

Applies to all workers, key personnel, volunteers and contractors of [Provider Name], across the services named in our registration. [Adjust if narrower.]

Policy statements

  1. Each participant directs their own supports; we fit around their choices, not the reverse.
  2. Culture, identity, values and beliefs are respected in how supports are delivered.
  3. We communicate in each participant's preferred language, mode and pace.
  4. We actively support access to advocates, interpreters and communication aids.
