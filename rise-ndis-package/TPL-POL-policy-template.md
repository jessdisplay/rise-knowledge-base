## 5. Policy statements

[The commitments. Numbered, one idea per statement, ≤ 20 words each where possible,
"We will / We do not" voice. These are the citable units — an auditor or a worker can
point to "POL-INC-01, statement 3".]

*Example set:*

1. *The safety and wellbeing of participants comes before every other consideration
   when an incident occurs.*
2. *We record every incident, including near misses, in the Incident Register.*
3. *We notify the NDIS Commission of reportable incidents within the timeframes set by
   the Incident Rules, using our Reportable Incidents Notification Procedure.*
4. *We support participants affected by an incident, including access to advocates and
   the information they need in a way they understand.*
5. *We review incidents to find causes and we act on what we learn.*
6. *We never punish anyone for raising an incident in good faith.*
