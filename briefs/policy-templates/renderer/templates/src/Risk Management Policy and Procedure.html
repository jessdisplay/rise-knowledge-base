1. Introduction

@standard Risk Management, NDIS Practice Standards @indicators Six, lettered (a) to (f) at 1.3 @register Policy Register: Version 1, approved October 2023

1.1 Purpose

This Policy and the Policies and Procedures and related documentation set out in section 1.5 below (Related Documentation) supports Harmony Care Support Services to apply the Risk Management NDIS Practice Standard.

1.2 Policy Aims

!! Harmony Care Support Services is committed to ensuring that risks to participants, workers and the provider are identified and managed..

1.3 NDIS Quality Indicators

In this regard, Harmony Care Support Services aims to demonstrate each of the following quality indicators through the application of this Policy and the relevant systems, procedures, workflows and other strategies referred to in this Policy and the Related Documentation: %% (a) Risks to the organisation, including risks to participants, financial and work health and safety risks, and risks associated with provision of support are identified, analysed, prioritised and treated. %% (b) A documented risk management system that effectively manages identified risks is in place, and is relevant and proportionate to the size and scale of the provider and the scope and complexity of support provided. %% (c) The risk management system covers each of the following: %% (1) incident management; %% (2) complaints management and resolution; %% (3) financial management; %% (4) governance and operational management; %% (5) human resource management; %% (6) information management; %% (7) work health and safety; and %% (8) emergency and disaster management. %% (d) Where relevant, the risk management system includes measures for the prevention and control of infection and outbreaks. %% (e) Supports and services are provided in a way that is consistent with the risk management system. %% (f) Appropriate insurance is in place, including professional indemnity, public liability and accident insurance.

1.4 Scope

%% (a) This Policy applies to the provision of all services and supports at Harmony Care Support Services. %% (b) All permanent, fixed term and casual staff, contractors and volunteers are required to take full responsibility for ensuring full understanding of the commitments outlined in this Policy. %% (c) The relevant persons specified in the column corresponding to a procedure described in this Policy have the responsibility to implement the relevant systems, procedures, workflows and other strategies referred to in the relevant procedure.

1.5 Related Documentation

The application of the above NDIS Practice Standard by Harmony Care Support Services is supported in part by and should be read alongside the Policies and Procedures and related documentation corresponding to this Policy in the Policy Register.

3. Policy Statement

@evidence Risk Register · Business Continuity Plan @law WHS Act 2011 (QLD) and WHS Regulation 2011 (QLD) %% (a) Risk Management in respect of risks to the organisation, including risks to Clients, financial and work health and safety risks and risks associated with the provision of support and services is an essential part of Harmony Care Support Services’s culture. %% (b) Harmony Care Support Services does what is reasonably practicable to ensure its Workers, consumers and other people are not harmed by the risks associated with the provision of support and services and commits sufficient resources to effectively manage risks. %% (c) This Risk Management Policy and the Related Documentation require Harmony Care Support Services to conduct Risk Management processes within its Workplace(s), across all aspects of its organisation and in relation to its operations including the provision of its supports and services. %% (d) Those Policies incorporate this Risk Management Policy by reference when the Principal or Workers undertake such a Risk Management process. It is expected that those Risk Management activities will be undertaken in a manner consistent with the Procedures set out below. %% (e) Risks and the plans to manage them are documented in the Risk Register. In addition, the Business Continuity Plan ensures the continuation of business during and following any critical incident that results in disruption to normal operational capability. %% (f) Work Health and Safety Risks are managed in accordance with the Harmony Care Support Services Work Health and Safety Policy. %% (g) Harmony Care Support Services is committed to continuous improvement in Risk Management. Internal Audits review the effectiveness of controls planned and implemented to manage risks in accordance with this Risk Management Policy.

4. Procedure

@who Principal · All Workers @records Named on each step below

The following procedures are to be applied at Harmony Care Support Services to provide guidance regarding Risk Management, to support the achievement of business objectives, protect Workers and business assets and to ensure financial sustainability. The Procedures work together dynamically and are relevant to all parts of Harmony Care Support Services. The Procedures are not ordered in priority and all are important to achieving the aims of the Policy Statement.

Risk Management Plan for identified risks | Principal | Risk Register - The Risk Register details strategies for dealing with identified risks to Harmony Care Support Services. - Appropriate control measures set out in the Risk Register should be actioned to ensure identified hazards are eliminated or minimised.

Business Continuity Plan and Critical Incident Management | | Business Continuity, Emergency and Disaster Preparedness Management Plan - The Business Continuity Plan ensures the continuation of business during and following any critical incident that results in disruption to normal operational capability.

Review of Risk Register and Business Continuity Plan | Principal | Business Continuity, Emergency and Disaster Preparedness Management Plan · Risk Register - The Risk Register and Business Continuity Plan should be reviewed contemporaneously with a Scheduled Review of this Policy and Procedures by following these four steps: - identify risks – undertake a review of Harmony Care Support Services to identify potential risks - assess risks – understand the likelihood of the risks causing harm and the potential consequences or impact if the risk eventuated - manage and control risks – manage risks involves avoiding, reducing, transferring or accepting the risk - monitor and review - regularly monitor and review the risk management plan to ensure the control measures and insurance cover is adequate.

Maintain Insurances | Principal | - Obtain and maintain an adequate level of insurance including Public Liability insurance, Professional Indemnity Insurance and Workers Compensation Insurance. - Insurances should be reviewed contemporaneously with a Scheduled Review of this Plan.

Welcoming feedback in relation to Risk Management | Principal | - Create an environment where all feedback is valued including from Clients, Workers and others to identify areas where Harmony Care Support Services can improve its Risk Management processes and procedures. - Welcome feedback (including anonymously) and promptly deal with any complaints pursuant to the Feedback and Complaints Management and Resolution Policy. - Actively consult with Workers, Clients, their support networks and other stakeholders to continually improve Risk Management processes at Harmony Care Support Services. - Conduct an annual survey of all Workers, Clients, their support networks and other stakeholders and ask them to suggest areas for improvement in relation to the Risk Management processes of Harmony Care Support Services.

Reporting of hazards and incidents | All Workers | Incident Management and Reporting Policy - Workers must report all incidents as soon as reasonably practicable including: - injuries to clients or workers; - emergency situations; and - near-miss incidents where there is no injury but requires preventative action. - All incidents will be managed in accordance with Harmony Care Support Services’s Incident Management System. - All incident investigations should include a review of the Risk Management Plan.

Emergency and Disaster Management | Principal and Key Management Personnel | Client Emergency Plan - ensuring that appropriate operational readiness measures are developed, implemented and reviewed. - prepare, test and annually review an Emergency Plan in respect of each of the residences and buildings where it works - actively monitoring each of the residences and buildings that it works in to maintain adherence to relevant building local laws, regulations or legislation, including provisions that apply retrospectively with respect to emergency readiness and safety measures. - doing what is reasonably practicable to improve the safety of vulnerable people in emergencies, through ensuring the undertaking of personal emergency planning. - ensuring that fire equipment is installed, suitable for risks specific to the relevant residence or building

Infection Control | Principal and Key Management Personnel | - Risks of infection are regularly assessed, identified and managed. - Employees are trained in infection control practice, including relevant application of precautions to minimise the risk of infection. - Mechanisms are in place for monitoring compliance with infection control procedures.

Checking for new information when it becomes available | Principal | - Where new information about risks in the context of Harmony Care Support Services’s operations and support provision becomes available, this information should be incorporated (where relevant) into the Risk Management Plan. - New information and advice about business risks should be confirmed with insurers and advisors.

Workers to commit to Policy | All Workers | - All Workers are provided with a copy of this policy in their orientation and induction materials. - Under their employment, contractor agreement or binding letter agreement, each Worker at Harmony Care Support Services is required to take responsibility for ensuring: - full understanding of the commitments outlined in this policy as well as procedures and other strategies designed to ensure that the principles of this policy are upheld; and - ensuring that the principles and procedures and other strategies within this Policy are applied in their daily work.

Train Workers | Principal | Risk Management Policy - Train Workers in the Risk Management Policy and Risk Management process during their induction, and as part of ongoing refresher training and/or when processes change of Workers with a need to know.

Continuous Improvement and Quality Management | Principal | Continuous Improvement Register - In addition to the continuous improvement and quality management procedures set out above, the following Continuous Improvement and Quality Management measures should be implemented contemporaneously with a Scheduled Review of this Policy and Procedures: - analyse relevant legislation for any changes to the requirements of this Policy and Procedures and to determine new areas of potential risk. - analyse records of incidents, near misses and complaints to identify new areas of potential risk. - review this Policy in consultation with other Key Management Personnel.

Policy adoption | Principal | - Adopt and maintain the Policy and Related Documentation which assists Harmony Care Support Services to demonstrate the relevant NDIS Quality Indicators related to the Risk Management NDIS Practice Standard.
