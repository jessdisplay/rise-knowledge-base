Purpose

This policy states [Provider Name]'s commitments for early childhood supports, meeting M3-1 The child; M3-2 The family.

Scope

Applies to all workers, key personnel, volunteers and contractors of [Provider Name], across the services named in our registration. [Adjust if narrower.]

Policy statements

  1. Our early childhood supports are family-centred, inclusive and evidence-informed.
  2. A key worker approach coordinates supports around the child and family.
  3. Supports build family and community capacity and happen in natural settings where possible.
  4. We measure outcomes that matter to the child and family.
