## Policy statements

1. Anyone - workers, participants, families, contractors - can report suspected wrongdoing, including anonymously.
2. We do not tolerate reprisals against anyone who speaks up in good faith.
3. A discloser's identity is protected; revealing it without consent is itself a breach.
4. Reports are assessed promptly and investigated in proportion to what is alleged.
5. Our settings follow the strengthened whistleblower protections introduced in 2026 - obtain legal review of the final wording before approval.
