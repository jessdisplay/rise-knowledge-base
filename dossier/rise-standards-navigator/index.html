The source documents (the "copies")

Rise links to the living law rather than duplicating it — copies go stale, and this framework has already been amended twice in 2026. Authoritative sources:

  • NDIS Act 2013 (Cth) — the foundation law both instruments are made under.
  • Provider Registration and Practice Standards Rules 2018 — current text · download the authorised PDF, Compilation No. 4 · series page (register ID F2018L00631; the standards live in Schedules 1–8).
  • Quality Indicators Guidelines 2018 — current text · Compilation No. 1 full text (register ID F2018N00041; notifiable instrument, made under s 181D(2) of the Act).
  • NDIS Commission — Practice Standards overview · all rules and policies.

Register links open at document level; the register does not offer verified section-level anchors, so use the address stamp (e.g. "Sch 7 · cl 4") to locate the exact clause inside the document.

What is verified and what is not

  • Verified: every standard name, both instrument titles where they differ, every address pairing (from the eleven statutory mapping tables), module applicability (from the section 20(3) registration table), and the reverse-order verification module quirk.
  • Paraphrase: the plain-English lines are faithful summaries written for this tool — they are not the legal text and never substitute for it.
  • Absent: the 2026 supported independent living standards, and any amendments after the compilation dates above.
