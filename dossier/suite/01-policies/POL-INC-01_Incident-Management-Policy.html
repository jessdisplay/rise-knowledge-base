Purpose

This policy states [Provider Name]'s commitments for incident management, meeting CORE-2.6 Incident management; VER-2 Incident management.

Scope

Applies to all workers, key personnel, volunteers and contractors of [Provider Name], across the services named in our registration. [Adjust if narrower.]

Policy statements

  1. The safety and wellbeing of participants comes before every other consideration when an incident occurs.
  2. We record every incident, including near misses, in REG-INC-01.
  3. We notify the NDIS Commission of reportable incidents within the timeframes set by the Incident Rules, using PRO-INC-02.
  4. We support participants affected by an incident, including advocates and information they understand.
  5. We review incidents to find causes and act on what we learn.
  6. We never punish anyone for raising an incident in good faith.
