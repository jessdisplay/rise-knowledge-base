Policy commitments

§3.1 — We uphold each participant's legal and human rights in everyday practice.

§3.2

We communicate with every participant about their supports in the language, mode and terms they are most likely to understand, and we record each person's communication preferences at intake and whenever they change.

§3.3 — We support each participant to engage the family, friends and community connections they choose.

3 · Procedure — Intake & communication profile

PRO-SEC-001 · example document

Step 1 — Welcome the participant; confirm consent to collect their information.

Step 2

Ask the participant (and, where they wish, their supporters) how they prefer to communicate — language, mode, format and terms. Record the answers in the communication profile section of the intake form.

Step 3 — File the completed form; the register updates from it.
