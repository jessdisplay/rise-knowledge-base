## Purpose

This policy states [Provider Name]'s commitments for human resource management, meeting CORE-2.7 Human resource management; VER-1 Human resource management.

## Scope

Applies to all workers, key personnel, volunteers and contractors of [Provider Name], across the services named in our registration. [Adjust if narrower.]

## Policy statements

1. Workers are screened before starting risk-assessed roles, with an NDIS Worker Screening Check verified and recorded.
2. Every worker is inducted on the NDIS Code of Conduct, safeguarding and the parts of this suite they use.
3. Workers are trained and assessed as competent for the supports they deliver, recorded in REG-HRM-02.
4. Workers receive regular supervision and honest performance feedback.
5. Only workers with current, assessed competence perform high intensity supports or administer medication.
