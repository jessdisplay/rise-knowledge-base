## Purpose

This policy states [Provider Name]'s commitments for continuity of supports, meeting CORE-2.8 Continuity of supports.

## Scope

Applies to all workers, key personnel, volunteers and contractors of [Provider Name], across the services named in our registration. [Adjust if narrower.]

## Policy statements

1. Participants keep receiving their supports through disruptions, planned or not.
2. Worker absences are covered by planned backup arrangements participants know about.
3. We maintain and test a business continuity plan (PLN-COS-01).
4. Participants are told early and clearly when changes affect them.
