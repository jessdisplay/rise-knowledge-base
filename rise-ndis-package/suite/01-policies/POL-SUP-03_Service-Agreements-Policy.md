## Scope

Applies to all workers, key personnel, volunteers and contractors of [Provider Name], across the services named in our registration. [Adjust if narrower.]

## Policy statements

1. A written service agreement is in place before or as supports start.
2. Agreements are in plain language, with Easy Read available, and the participant keeps a copy.
3. Agreements cover the supports, costs, how to change things, and how either party can end them.
4. Changes are agreed, not imposed.
